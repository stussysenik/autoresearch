## Key Results (TL;DR)

- **$\mu = 0.425 \pm 0.081$** across 3 Red Bull BC One dancers (lil g, Neguin, Morris)
- **41× separation** from random-motion controls ($p < 0.001$, Cohen's $d = 4.15$)
- **8 validated experiments** + 6 sensitivity sweeps
- **TRIVIUM scoring**: Body (40%) / Soul (35%) / Mind (25%) — aligned with WDSF criteria
- **96-dimensional move embeddings** for vector similarity knowledge pool
- **5 capture recipes** from iPhone solo ($0) to broadcast multi-cam ($8K+)
- **Community-first**: BreakDex as the dictionary layer, opt-in knowledge pool, cultural governance
